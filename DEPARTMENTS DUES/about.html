Purpose

Purpose of the system

This system is designed to allow university departments to manage student dues in a transparent, academic, and secure manner. It supports financial tracking, payment verification, notifications, receipt issuance, and reporting by department, programme, and level.

Students can self-serve dues information and payments, while treasurers and administrators can review, verify, and manage records. The project is designed to be professional, academic, and adaptable to a real university environment.

Transparency

Students can view dues, invoice breakdowns, payment statuses, and outstanding balances in one place.

Verification

Payments are verified before status updates and official receipts are generated.

Reporting

Reports support department-level and academic-structure analysis across programmes and levels.
